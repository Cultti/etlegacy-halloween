# Weathered equipment draft prompts

Built-in ImageGen edits of original game UV atlases extracted from the operator-owned pak0.pk3 into ignored build/equipment-draft/references/. Selected generated PNGs are saved beside this file. The exporter preserves original dimensions and material alpha byte-for-byte; no original game models or source textures are copied into the preview PK3. Original game artwork rights remain with their owners.

Initial blood/weathering requests for dynamite, knife and landmine were rejected by the tool; those originals remain unchanged. The stick-grenade variant uses rust and dirt without blood after its initial blood edit was rejected. This is a local preview, not a deployed or committed release.
